Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about your Todo App.

How do I create a new task?
Open your Todo dashboard, click the option to add a task, enter the required details, and save it.
How can I edit an existing task?
Find the task you want to update and click its Edit button. Change the details and submit the form to save your changes.
How do I delete a task?
Find the task you want to remove and click its Delete button. Check carefully before deleting because recovery may not be available.
Where are my tasks stored?
Your tasks are stored using the database configured for this application. Their persistence depends on your database and hosting configuration.
Can I use this app on my mobile phone?
Yes, this help page is responsive. Your dashboard will also need responsive styling for a good mobile experience.
What should I do if something goes wrong?
Check that you have entered the correct information. If the problem continues, use the support options below to report the issue.
